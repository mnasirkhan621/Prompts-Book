# Universal Quick-Reference Cheat Sheet

One-page cheat sheet for rapid copy-paste prompting during live video and music generation sessions.

---

## 🎬 Video Generation Prompt Cheat Sheet

### The Core Video Skeleton:
```text
Model: [Kling 3.0 / Seedance 2.0 / Runway Gen-3] | Aspect: [16:9 / 9:16 / 2.35:1] | Duration: [5s–10s]

[Subject + Physical Anchors + Specific Attire].
[Environment + Lighting Conditions + Volumetric Atmosphere].
[Single Primary Action + Observable Physical Movement].
Camera: [Named Preset] from [Start Framing] to [End Framing] over [X] seconds, [Focal Length] [Aperture].
Look: [Lighting Scheme], [Color Grade Palette], [Film Stock / Anamorphic Flaring].
Audio: [Spoken Dialogue in Quotes], [Ambient Tone], [Synchronized Sound Effects].
```

### Essential Camera Presets:
* **Dolly In**: Smooth forward linear track (`Camera Dolly In toward her eyes`)
* **Dolly Out**: Smooth backward pull (`Camera Dolly Out revealing the desolate ruins`)
* **FPV Drone**: High-velocity aerial weave (`FPV Drone weaves through alley behind the motorcycle`)
* **Action Run**: Low-angle ground follow (`Action Run — low angle, tracked 1m behind his sprint`)
* **Dutch Angle**: Off-axis diagonal tilt (`Dutch Angle canted 25 degrees, claustrophobic framing`)
* **360 Orbit**: Full circular rotation (`360 Orbit around the hero drawing the blade`)
* **Whip Pan**: High-speed motion blur swipe (`Whip Pan right from the gunshot to the broken window`)

## 🎵 Suno AI Music Prompt Cheat Sheet

### The Core Style Box Skeleton (Max 950 chars):
```text
[Era] + [Genre] + [Mood] + [3-4 Key Instruments] + [Vocal Style] + [Production Aesthetic] + [BPM: XXX] + [Key: X Minor/Major]
```
*Quick Example:*
`1980s dark synthwave, moody and driving, arpeggiated analog synth, punchy LinnDrum, detached baritone male vocals, tape saturation, BPM: 122, Key: A Minor`

### Essential Lyric Box Metatags:
```text
[Intro - Atmospheric synth pads]
[Verse 1 - Intimate dry vocals, minimalist bassline]
[Pre-Chorus - Rising drums, building harmonies]
[Chorus - Anthemic, full instrumentation, wall of sound]
[Post-Chorus - Catchy vocal chant]
[Verse 2 - Driving beat continues]
[Chorus - Maximum energy]
[Bridge - Tempo slows, stripped back to solo piano]
[Guitar Solo - Emotional soaring bends]
[Chorus - Explosive final chorus]
[Outro - Decelerating beat, fading synth echo]
[End - Final chord ring out, sudden cut]
```

### Essential Inline Vocal Cues:
* `[Whispered]` — Breathy, close-mic, intimate
* `[Spoken Word]` — Rhythmic speech delivery
* `[Falsetto]` — High head-voice register
* `[Powerhouse Vocals]` — Full chest-voice belting
* `[Stacked Harmonies]` — Wide choral vocal spread
