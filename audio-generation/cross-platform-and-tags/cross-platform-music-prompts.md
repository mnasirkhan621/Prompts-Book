## 🌐 Why Cross-Platform?

Different AI music platforms have different strengths. Suno excels at full vocal songs. Udio produces higher-fidelity audio. MusicGen is open source and runs locally. Stable Audio is great for sound effects and loops.

Knowing how to prompt across platforms makes you a versatile AI music creator. You can use the right tool for each job, or combine outputs from multiple platforms for hybrid production.

## 🔧 Cross-Platform Prompt Tips

### What Works Everywhere
- **Genre tags** (pop, rock, trap, house)
- **Mood descriptors** (melancholic, euphoric, aggressive)
- **Instrument names** (be specific: "jangly guitar" not "guitar")
- **BPM and key**
- **Production terminology** (reverb, saturation, compression)

### Platform-Specific Differences

| Element | Suno | Udio | MusicGen | Stable Audio |
|---------|------|------|----------|-------------|
| Vocals | Yes (full songs) | Yes (full songs) | Instrumental focus | No vocals |
| Max length | ~4 min | ~2 min (extendable) | 30 sec (varies) | Short clips |
| Structure control | Arrow notation | Less control | Limited | N/A |
| Best for | Full songs | Audio quality | Local/custom | SFX, loops |

### Adapting a Prompt Across Platforms

**Suno prompt:**
```
synthwave, analog synth, driving drum machine, gated reverb,
nostalgic mood, breathy vocals, 118 BPM, A minor
```

**Same concept for Udio:**
```
1980s synthwave production, analog Prophet synthesizer textures,
gated snare drum with heavy reverb, nostalgic cinematic atmosphere,
airy female vocals with lush harmonies, 118 BPM, A minor
```

**Same concept for MusicGen:**
```
synthwave instrumental, analog synthesizer lead, drum machine with gated reverb,
nostalgic 1980s atmosphere, 118 BPM, A minor key
```
