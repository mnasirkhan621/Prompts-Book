## 🤔 What Are Style Tags?

Style tags are descriptive keywords and phrases you feed to Suno AI to control the sonic character of your generated music. They're the building blocks of every great Suno prompt. Instead of naming an artist directly (which triggers copyright blocks), style tags describe the **sonic DNA** of a sound.

**Weak prompt:** "Make a song like Drake"
→ Copyright blocked or generic output.

**Strong prompt:** `introspective rap, melancholic piano, trap hi-hats, deep 808 bass, whispered confessional vocals, 75 BPM`
→ Specific, copyright-safe, chart-quality result.

This dictionary helps you find the right tags for any sound you're chasing.
