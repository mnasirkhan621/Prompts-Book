# Song Structure Metatags Catalog

Structural metatags are bracketed directives placed on their own lines inside the **Lyrics Box**. They instruct Suno's arrangement engine when to shift dynamics, change instrumentation, trigger drops, and introduce vocals.

---

## Core Arrangement Metatags

| Metatag | Placement | Function | Recommended Accompanying Directives |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `[Intro]` | Start of track | Establishes the groove and musical theme | `[Intro - Fingerpicked acoustic guitar]` or `[Slow Ambient Intro]` |
| `[Verse 1]` | Early track | Primary narrative, lower energy, clear lyric focus | `[Verse 1 - Driving bass, dry vocals]` |
| `[Pre-Chorus]` | Between Verse & Chorus | Builds rhythmic and melodic tension | `[Pre-Chorus - Rising snare, vocal octave climb]` |
| `[Chorus]` | Climax of section | Primary melodic hook, maximum energy and layers | `[Chorus - Anthemic, full instrumentation, wall of sound]` |
| `[Post-Chorus]` | Directly after Chorus | Secondary hook, instrumental riff or vocal chant | `[Post-Chorus - Repetitive vocal chop]` |
| `[Bridge]` | After 2nd Chorus | Contrasting key, tempo, or emotional angle | `[Bridge - Stripped down, solo piano, emotive]` |
| `[Drop]` | In EDM / Electronic | Sudden bass release following a build-up | `[Drop - Distorted 808, aggressive bassline]` |
| `[Instrumental Break]` | Mid-track | Complete pause on vocals, focus on lead instrument | `[Instrumental Break - Saxophone solo]` or `[Guitar Solo]` |
| `[Outro]` | End of track | Deceleration, resolution, winding down | `[Outro - Fading synths, lingering reverb trail]` |
| `[End]` | Final line | Forces an immediate definitive stop or ring-out | `[Big Finish, Sudden Stop]` or `[End]` |

---

## Example Full Song Lyric Structure Sheet

```text
[Intro - Soft electric piano and distant atmospheric vinyl crackle]

[Verse 1 - Intimate dry vocals, minimalist bassline]
Late night headlights sweep across the ceiling
Counting down the shadows on the wall
Quiet moments amplify the feeling
Wondering who will be the first to call

[Pre-Chorus - Rising drums, building synth harmonies]
The tension is rising, the dial is turned
Every lesson we never learned

[Chorus - Powerful vocal delivery, layered harmonies, driving drums]
We are moving through the neon rain
Washing away the static and the pain
Hold on to the frequency we found
Before the morning turns it all around

[Post-Chorus - Catchy vocal hook with synth arpeggios]
Neon rain, falling down
Neon rain, lost and found

[Verse 2 - Drums remain active, guitars enter]
City sirens fade into the distance
Pavement mirrors reflecting every light
Breaking through the stubborn cold resistance
Carving out our corner of the night

[Chorus - Maximum energy, anthemic backing vocals]
We are moving through the neon rain
Washing away the static and the pain
Hold on to the frequency we found
Before the morning turns it all around

[Bridge - Tempo slows, stripped back to acoustic guitar and solo voice]
And if the signal breaks apart
We still possess the rhythm in our heart

[Guitar Solo - Emotional high notes, soaring bends with stereo delay]

[Chorus - Explosive final chorus, wall of sound]
We are moving through the neon rain!
Washing away the static and the pain!
Hold on to the frequency we found
Before the morning turns it all around

[Outro - Decelerating beat, vocals fade into echo]
Neon rain... fading out...
Frequency remains...

[End - Final chord ring out, sudden cut]
```
