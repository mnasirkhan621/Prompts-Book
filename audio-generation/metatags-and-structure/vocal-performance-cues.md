# Inline Vocal Performance Metatags

Vocal performance metatags are bracketed directives inserted directly into the lyrics lines or attached to section tags. They control the singer's delivery style, vocal range, emotional intensity, and audio effects.

---

## 1. Vocal Delivery & Emotional Register Cues

Insert these inline or as sub-tags:

| Tag | Placement | Audio Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `[Whispered]` | Directly before phrase | Intimate, close-mic, breathy delivery with low vocal volume |
| `[Spoken Word]` | Before verse or section | Rhythmic spoken cadence, non-melodic delivery |
| `[Falsetto]` | Above high emotional phrase | Head-voice register, delicate and airy |
| `[Belting]` / `[Powerhouse]` | Over chorus climax | Chest-voice high volume, full resonance |
| `[Gravelly]` / `[Raspy]` | Start of section | Distorted, textured vocal cords, blues/rock grit |
| `[Scream]` / `[Growl]` | Metal or hardcore tracks | Aggressive guttural scream or fry vocal |
| `[Vocal Fry]` | End of phrases | Low register creaking vocal texture |

---

## 2. Vocal Harmony & Arrangement Tags

| Tag | Placement | Audio Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `[Harmonies]` | Chorus lines | Adds tight 3rd and 5th interval harmonies |
| `[Stacked Harmonies]` | Anthemic moments | Large choral or multi-tracked vocal layers |
| `[Call and Response]` | Duet or verse lines | Lead vocal followed by immediate group echo |
| `[Ad-libs]` | Background margins | Background vocal riffs, shouts, and melodic embellishments |
| `(Choir)` or `[Gospel Choir]` | Climax section | Expansive choir backing harmonies |

---

## 3. Inline Syntax Examples

### Example 1: Dynamic Verse to Chorus Transition
```text
[Verse 1: Spoken word, dry close-mic]
Walking through the empty streets at four AM.
Footsteps echoing off the glass and steel.

[Pre-Chorus: Building energy | Rising pitch]
[Whispered] Can you hear it?
[Normal] The sound is getting louder now.

[Chorus: Powerhouse belting vocals, stacked harmonies, wide stereo]
WE LIGHT THE SKY TONIGHT!
[Falsetto] Above the highest tower...
WE NEVER LOSE THE LIGHT!
```

### Example 2: Ad-libs & Call-and-Response
```text
[Verse 1: Male Rap | Fast flow]
Running up the numbers like an algorithm (yeah, yeah!)
Never looking back at what we left behind (let's go!)
Breaking through the ceiling of the old design (uh-huh)
```
