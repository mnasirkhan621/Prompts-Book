# The 7-Element Suno Prompt Anatomy

Every high-performing Suno AI music prompt is built from a strategic combination of **7 Core Elements**. While not every element is required in every prompt, understanding each component allows you to sculpt exact acoustic results rather than relying on random generation.

---

## The 7 Core Elements

| # | Element | What It Controls | Example Syntax | Purpose |
| :-: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Genre & Era** | The foundational musical style and decade | `1980s synth-pop`, `late-90s boom bap`, `modern cinematic ambient` | Grounds the model in a specific production era and groove. |
| **2** | **Mood & Atmosphere** | The emotional frequency and psychological weight | `melancholic`, `defiant`, `euphoric`, `dark brooding` | Steers chord progressions and melodic modes (minor vs. major). |
| **3** | **Instrumentation** | The exact physical sound palette and instruments | `analog synthesizers, 808 sub-bass, live acoustic drums, jangly guitar` | Replaces generic synthesized defaults with concrete timbres. |
| **4** | **Vocal Character** | Vocal timbre, delivery, gender, and technique | `raspy baritone male vocal`, `airy female contralto`, `whispered delivery` | Prevents generic auto-tuned pop vocals from overpowering the track. |
| **5** | **Production Aesthetic** | Studio processing, acoustic environment, and mixing | `tape saturation, lo-fi cassette hiss, stadium reverb, dry close-mic` | Determines whether the song sounds like a basement demo or an arena master. |
| **6** | **Technical Specs** | Tempo and harmonic key constraints | `BPM: 124, Key: D Minor` | Locks rhythmic speed and prevents unnatural tempo drift. |
| **7** | **Structure Directives** | Song arrangement hints in style box | `progressive build, dynamic chorus explosion, sudden stop` | Pre-conditions the arrangement flow before lyrics are applied. |

---

## The Master Style Prompt Formula

```text
[Era] + [Core Genre] + [Mood] + [3-4 Key Instruments] + [Vocal Character] + [Production Aesthetic] + [BPM: XXX] + [Key: X Minor/Major]
```

### Production Examples:

#### 1. Cyberpunk Synthwave:
```text
1980s dark synthwave, moody and driving, arpeggiated analog bassline, punchy LinnDrum, warm Juno-106 pads, detached baritone male vocals, subtle tape saturation, stereo chorus on guitars, BPM: 120, Key: A Minor
```

#### 2. Indie Folk Acoustic:
```text
Contemporary indie folk, bittersweet and intimate, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, upright bass, warm cello swells, breathy female vocals with delicate vibrato, dry close-mic production, subtle room acoustics, BPM: 88, Key: G Major
```

#### 3. Modern Melodic Trap:
```text
Modern dark trap, aggressive and menacing, distorted 808 sub-bass, rapid hi-hat rolls, dark minor piano chords, autotuned melodic male vocals, crisp modern mix, heavy sidechain compression, BPM: 140, Key: F Minor
```
