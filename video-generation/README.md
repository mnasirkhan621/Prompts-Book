## 🏛️ Overview

The **Video Generation Engine** of **Prompts Book** bridges high-end film direction with modern generative video diffusion models (Kling 3.0, Seedance 2.0/4K, Runway Gen-3, OpenAI Sora, Luma Dream Machine).
