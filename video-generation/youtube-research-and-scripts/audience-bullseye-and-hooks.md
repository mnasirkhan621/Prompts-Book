## 1. The 5-Ring Audience Bullseye (`bullseye` skill)

Chasing viral breadth destroys long-term channel conversion because casual viewers never return for niche uploads. Map your audience across **5 concentric rings** centered on your core buyer/viewer:

```text
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  Ring 1: Broad General Interest (AVOID - empty vanity impressions)    |
|   └─ Ring 2: Adjacent Curiosity (Tech / Productivity / Creators)      |
|       └─ Ring 3–4: Tool & Craft Practitioners (Editors / AI Builders) |
|           └─ Ring 5 (CENTER): Exact Core Niche & High-Intent Audience |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### The `3-2-2` Weekly Content Mix
Across every 7 uploads, enforce strategic reach without losing channel authority:
* **3 Videos in Ring 5 (Core Niche):** Deep authority builders that convert superfans and buyers.
* **2 Videos in Rings 3–4 (Practitioner Bridge):** Workflow tutorials and tool comparisons welcoming adjacent creators.
* **2 Videos in Ring 2 (Controlled Expansion):** Accessible concepts connecting your core expertise to wider creator desires.
* **0 Videos in Ring 1:** Never publish generic clickbait untethered from your channel's core value proposition.

## 2. The Triangle of Non-Redundancy

Winning videos never waste real estate repeating the same phrase across packaging and opening seconds:

```text
   THUMBNAIL TEXT/VISUAL  ──(Sparks visual curiosity / contrast)──┐
   VIDEO TITLE            ──(Names clear stakes / promise)        ├──▶  OPEN LOOP
   SPOKEN + VISUAL HOOK   ──(Escalates immediate tension < 15s) ──┘
```

If your title says *"5 AI Video Prompts That Changed My Workflow"*, your thumbnail must **not** repeat those words, and your first spoken line must **not** say *"Here are 5 AI video prompts..."*.

## 3. Data-Backed Hook Machine Protocol (`hook-machine` skill)

Never write hooks from intuition when real outlier transcripts exist. Build a living rubric in `youtube-studio/brand/hook_rubric.md` from 15–30 verified outlier videos ($\ge 2\times$ median).

### 3.1 Six Proven Opening Archetypes
1. **Then vs. Now Contrast:** Establish a familiar past friction point, then pivot sharply in sentence two without spoiling the payoff.
2. **Contrarian Belief Challenge:** Directly challenge an unquestioned industry habit within the first 10 words.
3. **Result / Metric First:** Open mid-proof showing a concrete generated outcome, render, or chart before explaining how it was built.
4. **Immediate Shared Tension:** Name a frustration the viewer experienced earlier today (e.g., warped AI hands or dead-air pacing).
5. **Personal Test / Confession:** State an exact personal test performed under constraint (*"I ran the same scene across 5 video engines..."*).
6. **Story in Motion (In Media Res):** Drop straight into active conflict or troubleshooting, delaying background exposition until viewer buy-in locks.

### 3.2 Mandatory Non-Negotiable Hook Laws
* **Zero Preamble:** Ban all greetings (*"Hey guys"*, *"Welcome back"*, *"In today's video"*).
* **Under 15 Seconds / ~40 Words:** Reach the open question cleanly inside 40 spoken words (~8 words max for Shorts/Reels hooks).
* **2-Second Visual Change:** Introduce a concrete frame shift, UI proof, camera motion, or visual contrast within the first 2 seconds.
* **Delay the Answer:** Open a clear curiosity gap early; pay it off only in the main demonstration.
* **Creator Voice Fidelity:** Write in plain spoken rhythm (`youtube-studio/brand/about_me/voice.md`) stripped of marketing hype.
