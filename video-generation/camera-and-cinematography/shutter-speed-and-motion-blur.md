# Shutter Angles & Motion Blur Dynamics
### *The 180-Degree Rule and Kinetic Clarity in AI Video*
**Architect:** **Muhammad Nasir Khan**

Motion blur in video generation is determined by the virtual **shutter angle**. Specifying shutter characteristics prevents the sluggish, muddy smear common to unconstrained AI video generations.

---

## 1. Shutter Angle Standards

| Shutter Angle | Effective Shutter Speed @ 24fps | Kinetic Character | Ideal Use Case | Prompt Syntax |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **45° – 90°** | 1/192s – 1/96s | Ultra-crisp, staccato, zero smear | Combat action, water splashes, explosion debris | `Shutter angle 90 degrees, crisp motion clarity without smear` |
| **180°** | 1/48s | Natural human eye cinematic blur | Standard narrative dialogue, walking, drama | `Standard 180-degree shutter, natural cinematic motion blur` |
| **270° – 360°** | 1/32s – 1/24s | Dreamlike, elongated light streaks | Intoxication, dream sequences, light trails | `Low shutter 360 degrees, heavy motion blur and light trailing` |
