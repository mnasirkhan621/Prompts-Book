# Key-to-Fill Contrast Ratios for AI Cinematography
### *Lighting Ratios and Photometric Contrast Control*
**Architect:** **Muhammad Nasir Khan**

Lighting contrast in AI video diffusion models is controlled by the mathematical ratio between the **Key Light** (primary illumination) and the **Fill Light** (shadow fill). Specifying these ratios prevents flat, washed-out AI generations.

---

## 1. Contrast Ratio Standards

| Ratio (Key:Fill) | Contrast Level | Visual Mood | Best Genres | Prompt Specification Syntax |
| :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1:1** | Flat / Shadowless | Commercial high-key, clinical, sterile | Tech hardware, medical ads, cosmetics | `Look: High-key 1:1 shadowless lighting, pure white ambient bounce` |
| **2:1** | Subtle / Soft | Natural, friendly, open | Comedy, corporate interview, daylight lifestyle | `Look: Soft 2:1 key-to-fill ratio, gentle cheek shadow falloff` |
| **4:1** | Moderate / Dramatic | Realistic depth, cinematic narrative | Mainstream drama, documentary, romance | `Look: Classic 4:1 cinematic lighting, defined cheekbone shadow` |
| **8:1** | High / Dramatic | Moody, tense, dramatic separation | Thriller, crime noir, psychological drama | `Look: Chiaroscuro 8:1 contrast ratio, deep shadow on unlit side` |
| **16:1+** | Extreme Silhouette | Stark silhouettes, rim-only outlines | Mystery, horror, heroic back-lit reveals | `Look: Extreme 16:1 silhouette lighting, razor-sharp golden rim` |
