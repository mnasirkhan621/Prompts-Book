# Motion Design: Classic Motion (`classicMD`)

The **Classic Motion** workflow is designed for premium brand presentations, SaaS demos, explainers, and commercial promos. It prioritizes smooth spatial transitions, elegant typography zones, legible reading settles, and cinematic pacing.

---

## Visual Characteristics of Classic Motion

* **Typography**: Clean sans-serif or refined editorial serif typography placed in dedicated negative-space zones.
* **Transitions**: Smooth spatial pans, scale transitions, morphing UI elements, and continuous camera travel.
* **Reading Settles**: Deliberate holds (1.5s–2.5s) where movement stabilizes so viewers can comfortably absorb copy.
* **Lighting & Materials**: Matte pastel gradients, tactile clay, brushed aluminum, subtle glassmorphism, soft ambient occlusion.

---

## Production Worked Example: SaaS Product Launch

```text
Model: Seedance 2.0 / Kling 3.0 | Aspect: 16:9 | Duration: 10s

Smooth, premium SaaS product motion graphic.
Begins with a floating, frosted-glass dashboard widget displaying live animated analytics graphs.
Camera glides smoothly along a 45-degree isometric vector into the widget as data points pulse with soft emerald light.
The widget unfolds seamlessly into a clean multi-column workspace interface.
A 2-second settled hold allows clear reading of headline copy: "Intelligent Workflows. Zero Friction."
Camera pulls back with elegant ease-out deceleration to reveal the centered brand lockup: "NEXUS AI — The Future of Work".
Look: Refined minimalist studio lighting, soft diffused shadows, subtle slate-grey and deep navy gradient background.
Audio: Subtle modern electronic riser, gentle UI clicks, elegant synth chime on logo resolve.
```
