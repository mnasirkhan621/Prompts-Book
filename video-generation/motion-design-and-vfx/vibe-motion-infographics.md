# Vibe-Motion & Data-Driven Infographics
### *Animated Charts, Kinetic Typography & Code-Driven Motion Graphics*
**Architect:** **Muhammad Nasir Khan**

Vibe-Motion represents the intersection of code-driven animation (Remotion, SVG paths) and generative AI video. It focuses on crisp data visualization, metric counter tickers, and logo reveals.
