# Motion Design: Kinetic & Hyper Motion (`highMD`)

The **Hyper / Kinetic Motion** workflow is designed for extreme-energy tech releases, sports promos, gaming trailers, streetwear drops, and viral social hooks. It prioritizes peak dynamics, aggressive match-cuts, speed ramps, and high-impact visual rhythm.

---

## Visual Rules of Kinetic Hyper Motion

1. **No Realistic People**: Humans are stylized into silhouettes, chrome metallic figures, wireframes, or abstract 3D models.
2. **Extreme Camera Dynamics**: Whip pans, rapid FPV weaving, crash zooms, and sudden freeze-frame impacts.
3. **Materials & Contrast**: Neon emissive glow against deep pitch blacks, liquid chrome, shattered acrylic, high-contrast glitch effects.
4. **Proportional Hard-Stop Logo Lock**:
   The final seconds of the video MUST freeze into a rock-solid, static brand logo lock with zero camera drift:
   - **5-second clip**: 1.0 second static lock
   - **10-second clip**: 2.0 seconds static lock
   - **15-second clip**: 2.5–3.0 seconds static lock

---

## Standard Prompt Template

```text
Style: High-intensity kinetic motion graphic, [aggressive scene sequence], extreme camera speed,
aggressive match-cuts, peak-action freeze frames, [cyberpunk/hyper-tech] CGI aesthetic, high-contrast neon lighting.
Hard stop static logo lock for final [X] seconds: [Brand Name], centered, [aspect ratio].
```

## Production Worked Example: Streetwear Sneaker Drop

```text
Model: Seedance 2.0 / Kling 3.0 | Aspect: 9:16 | Duration: 10s

Hyper-kinetic streetwear teaser with extreme tempo.
Opening frame: A chrome-textured humanoid mannequin shatters into thousands of floating glass shards at 120fps.
Whip Pan into a floating holographic sneaker rotating frantically amidst flashing violet laser grids.
Aggressive speed-ramp: High-velocity zoom toward the sneaker heel, instantly freezing on impact at second 4.
Text flashes with rapid kinetic typography: "DROP 01 // 24 HOURS ONLY".
At second 8.0, all motion abruptly hard-stops into a dead-still static hold on the stark white logo: "CYBERPUNK X", black background, zero camera movement through second 10.
Look: High-contrast cyberpunk club aesthetic, deep obsidian blacks, blinding strobe flashes, neon cyan edge highlights.
Audio: Aggressive industrial phonk beat with distorted 808 bass, metallic impact drop, abrupt silence on the logo freeze.
```
