# Production Prompts: Music Videos & Dance Performance

High-energy prompt templates for choreographed dance routines, cyphers, stage lighting sync, and musical pacing.

---

## 1. Underground Hip-Hop Cypher

```text
Model: Kling 3.0 / Seedance 2.0 | Aspect: 16:9 | Duration: 6s | FPS: 30

A breakdancer in oversized streetwear executes an explosive windmill rotation on a cardboard sheet inside an underground concrete tunnel.
Surrounding dancers bob rhythmically to the beat, hyping the circle.
Camera: Low Ground-Level Handheld with fast 180-degree semi-orbit matching the dancer's rotational spin.
Look: Raw urban aesthetic, single tungsten floodlight casting harsh elongated shadows, airborne chalk dust motes.
Audio: Heavy sub-bass 808 kick, vinyl record scratch SFX, crowd cheers and rhythmic clapping.
```

## 2. Contemporary Dance Solo with Volumetric Haze

```text
Model: Kling 3.0 | Aspect: 16:9 | Duration: 8s | FPS: 24

A contemporary dancer in a flowing silk tunic performs an expressive, fluid leap across an empty dark theatre stage.
Dense theatrical haze fills the space; a single powerful overhead spotlight forms a cone of volumetric white light.
Her fabric trails behind her movement like liquid smoke as she lands and extends into an arch.
Camera: Smooth Crane Down descending from high oblique angle to eye level in sync with her landing.
Look: Minimalist high-contrast stage lighting, monochromatic palette with subtle warm skin tones, 50mm F2.0.
Audio: Resonant piano chords, soft intake of breath on the leap, gentle rustle of silk fabric.
```
