# Production Prompts: Action & Pursuit

Curated, high-fidelity prompt templates engineered for intense kinetics, dynamic choreography, and physical realism.

---

## 1. High-Speed Urban Foot Chase

```text
Model: Kling 3.0 | Aspect: 16:9 | Duration: 8s | FPS: 30

A female operative in a charcoal tactical jacket sprints full-tilt through a crowded night market.
She weaves between metal stalls, water splashing from wet tarpaulins.
Camera: Action Run — low angle, tracked 1.5 meters behind her matching sprint speed, shutter angle 180 degrees.
At second 4, she slides under a closing corrugated roller shutter, ducking low without breaking momentum.
Look: High-contrast cinematic, saturated neon magenta and amber streetlamp reflections, deep wet asphalt blacks.
Audio: Heavy rhythmic footfalls on wet pavement, startled vendor shouts, metallic rattle of the rolling gate.
```

## 2. Rooftop Combat & Standoff

```text
Model: Seedance 2.0 / Kling 3.0 | Aspect: 2.35:1 | Duration: 6s | FPS: 24

Two martial artists engage in hand-to-hand combat on a rain-swept high-rise rooftop at dusk.
The fighter in black parries a strike with forearm deflection and executes a sweeping leg kick.
Camera: Whip Pan right following the sweep kick, smoothly resolving into an Arc Shot circling 90 degrees around the fighters.
Look: Cold steel blue palette, rim lighting from city skyscraper windows in the background, volumetric wind-blown mist.
Audio: Sharp fabric whoosh of kicks, dull thud of blocked strikes, whistling high-altitude wind.
```

## 3. Underground Car Pursuit

```text
Model: Runway Gen-3 / Kling 3.0 | Aspect: 16:9 | Duration: 7s | FPS: 30

A matte-black sports coupe drifts hard around a concrete pillar inside an underground parking garage.
Tires smoke violently, leaving black rubber streaks on polished epoxy flooring.
Camera: Car Grip — low ground-level rig mounted 20 cm above pavement, shaking with mechanical engine vibration.
The vehicle straightens out and rockets directly past the lens with explosive acceleration.
Look: Overhead flickering fluorescent tubes, harsh specular streaks on car bodywork, dense tire smoke back-lit by headlights.
Audio: Screeching tire squeal, roaring V8 engine downshift, echoing tire smoke hiss.
```

## 4. Zero-Gravity Tactical Breach

```text
Model: Kling 3.0 / Seedance 2.0 | Aspect: 16:9 | Duration: 8s | FPS: 24

A heavily armored soldier floats through a breached bulkhead door into an airless, zero-gravity space station corridor.
Frozen coolant crystals and shattered composite fragments drift weightlessly around his visor.
He fires forward thrusters on his maneuvering pack to decelerate.
Camera: FPV Drone style slow glide, navigating through drifting debris directly ahead of the soldier.
Look: Stark single directional emergency floodlight, pitch black vacuum shadows, ultra-sharp reflections on gold visor.
Audio: Sub-bass hull vibration, muffled radio static breathing, silent vacuum environment.
```
