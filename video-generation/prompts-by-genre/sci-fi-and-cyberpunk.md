# Production Prompts: Sci-Fi & Cyberpunk

World-building prompt templates for futuristic architecture, high-tech cockpits, dystopian environments, and cybernetic interfaces.

---

## 1. Dystopian Cyberpunk Street Level

```text
Model: Kling 3.0 | Aspect: 16:9 | Duration: 8s | FPS: 24

A cybernetic courier wearing an illuminated holographic mask walks through a rain-drenched megacity alley.
Dozens of multi-story vertical neon signs in Japanese and English cast shifting cyan and violet light across puddles.
Flying vehicles (spinners) cruise slowly between skyscrapers in the hazy smog overhead.
Camera: Slow Dolly Out from medium shot, expanding to reveal the towering scale of the vertical city surrounding him.
Look: Rich cinematic neo-noir, deep blacks, high saturation neon highlights, heavy atmospheric rain fog. 35mm F2.8.
Audio: Ambient drone hum, distant siren echoes, steady rainfall on plastic tarps, deep low-frequency city rumble.
```

## 2. Starfighter Cockpit Emergency Re-entry

```text
Model: Seedance 2.0 / Kling 3.0 | Aspect: 2.35:1 | Duration: 6s | FPS: 30

A fighter pilot inside a pressurized starfighter cockpit battles severe atmospheric turbulence.
Outside the reinforced canopy glass, violent orange re-entry plasma flames roar across the exterior frame.
Inside the cockpit, red warning HUD telemetry reflects across the pilot's sweat-beaded visor.
Camera: Handheld camera with controlled high-frequency cockpit vibration, framing Medium Close-Up on the pilot's helmet.
Look: Intense fiery orange key light from exterior plasma, contrasting with cold blue interior console backlighting.
Audio: Deafening roar of atmospheric drag, piercing computer warning klaxon ("Altitude critical"), heavy pilot breathing.
```

## 3. Android Assembly & Diagnostics

```text
Model: Kling 3.0 / Runway Gen-3 | Aspect: 16:9 | Duration: 7s | FPS: 24

A minimalist, sterile white laboratory chamber.
An synthetic female android sits immobile on an ergonomic pedestal as precision robotic arms calibrate exposed carbon-fiber arm joints.
A faint line of luminescent white light scans vertically down her synthetic skin, rendering natural human texture.
Camera: 360 Orbit — smooth, continuous turntable sweep around the seated android at constant 1.5-meter distance.
Look: High-key clinical lighting, shadowless diffused white ceiling illumination, pure reflections on polished chrome surfaces.
Audio: Gentle electronic servo whirring, periodic high-pitched digital chime, sterile white noise.
```
