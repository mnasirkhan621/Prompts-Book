# Production Prompts: Drama & Cinematic Narrative

Master prompt templates for dialogue-driven narrative, emotional turning points, character intimacy, and human drama.

---

## 1. Intimate Cafe Conversation & Subtext

```text
Model: Kling 3.0 | Aspect: 16:9 | Duration: 8s | FPS: 24

Two colleagues sit across from each other at a small walnut table by a rain-streaked cafe window.
The woman leans forward slightly: "I found the missing ledger — you need to read this."
The man sets down his porcelain cup slowly, eyes fixed on hers: "Not here."
Camera: Over-The-Shoulder (OTS) Medium Close-Up from behind the man, framing the woman's nuanced facial expression.
Look: Warm incandescent cafe interior contrasting with cold overcast daylight through the glass. 50mm F2.0 lens.
Audio: Spoken dialogue with crisp dry acoustic balance, quiet ambient cafe murmur, distant rain patter on glass.
```

## 2. Solitary Hospital Corridor Vigil

```text
Model: Kling 3.0 / Seedance 2.0 | Aspect: 2.35:1 | Duration: 8s | FPS: 24

A tired surgeon in rumpled surgical scrubs sits alone on a plastic waiting room chair in an empty hospital hallway.
Her elbows rest on her knees, hands clasped, head bowed.
She slowly lifts her gaze toward the emergency doors at the end of the hall; eyes glassy with fatigue.
Camera: Slow Dolly In along the floor centerline from Wide Shot to Medium Close-Up over 8 seconds.
Look: Desaturated hospital green and pale fluorescent lighting, deep reflections on polished linoleum floor.
Audio: Low hum of industrial ventilation, rhythmic distant beep of a heart monitor, soft squeak of rubber shoes down the hall.
```

## 3. Harbour Dock Standoff at Dusk

```text
Model: Kling 3.0 | Aspect: 16:9 | Duration: 7s | FPS: 24

A solitary figure in a dark wool overcoat stands on a wooden pier overlooking a misty harbor at twilight.
A foghorn bellows in the distance; gentle waves lap rhythmically against mossy wooden pilings below.
The figure turns slowly in three-quarters profile, collar pulled high against the cold sea breeze.
Camera: Slow Crane Down from high oblique angle descending to eye-level profile over 7 seconds.
Look: Cold steel blue hour ambient light, warm sodium vapor glow from distant shipyard cranes, 2.35:1 anamorphic.
Audio: Distant maritime foghorn, gentle water lapping against wood, whistling coastal breeze.
```
