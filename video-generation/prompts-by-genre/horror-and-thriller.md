# Production Prompts: Horror & Psychological Thriller

Atmospheric prompt templates engineered for suspense, dread, uncanny encounters, and physiological fear triggers.

---

## 1. The Creeping Hallway Door

```text
Model: Kling 3.0 | Aspect: 16:9 | Duration: 7s | FPS: 24

An old residential hallway with faded damask wallpaper and worn parquet flooring.
At the far end of the darkened corridor, a heavy wooden bedroom door slowly, silently unlatches and creaks open 10 centimeters.
Dense black shadow looms inside the gap; faint silhouette movement is perceptible within the darkness.
Camera: Slow Dolly In creeping along the hallway carpet toward the cracked door at 10 cm per second.
Look: Chiaroscuro lighting, single bare warm tungsten bulb hanging from the ceiling, deep crushed pitch-black shadows.
Audio: High-pitched wood door creak, low ominous cello drone, dead acoustic silence.
```

## 2. Bathroom Mirror Disparity

```text
Model: Seedance 2.0 / Kling 3.0 | Aspect: 16:9 | Duration: 6s | FPS: 24

A young man leans over a porcelain bathroom sink with water running, splashing cold water onto his face.
He looks up directly into the medicine cabinet mirror.
As he lowers his hands and blinks, his reflection in the mirror remains motionless, unblinking, smiling faintly back at him.
Camera: Locked-off Eye Level Close-Up framing both the real profile and the full reflection in sharp focus.
Look: Sickly greenish-white fluorescent light overhead, condensation misting the edges of the mirror glass.
Audio: Steady water stream flowing into drain, sudden sharp discordant violin screech as the reflection smiles.
```

## 3. Flashlight Beam in Abandoned Basement

```text
Model: Kling 3.0 / Runway Gen-3 | Aspect: 16:9 | Duration: 7s | FPS: 24

POV first-person perspective holding a high-powered tactical LED flashlight inside an abandoned industrial basement.
The white beam cuts through floating dust motes, illuminating rusted metal pipes and damp concrete walls.
The light beam pans across the room and catches the pale, motionless figure of a humanoid entity crouched in the far corner.
Camera: Handheld POV camera, organic operator breathing, flashlight beam center-weighted.
Look: Harsh 6500K LED beam illumination surrounded by impenetrable absolute darkness, volumetric dust particulates.
Audio: Heavy ragged breathing of the operator, crunch of glass underfoot, abrupt sub-bass thud.
```
