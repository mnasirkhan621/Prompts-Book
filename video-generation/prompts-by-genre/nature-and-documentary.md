# Production Prompts: Nature & Wildlife Documentary

Documentary-grade prompt templates for cinematic landscapes, wildlife behaviors, extreme weather, and macro natural phenomena.

---

## 1. Arctic Snow Leopard Ridge Stalk

```text
Model: Kling 3.0 / Seedance 2.0 | Aspect: 16:9 | Duration: 8s | FPS: 30

A snow leopard with thick grey-rosetted fur stalks deliberately along a windswept mountain ridgeline in the Himalayas.
Snowflakes whip across the frame in heavy gusts; the leopard's amber eyes are locked intensely on prey down-slope.
Every muscle in its shoulders flexes with deliberate, silent predatory grace.
Camera: Telephoto Tracking Shot (400mm lens compression) tracking parallel with the leopard's forward march.
Look: High-key overcast mountain daylight, cold blue ice undertones, crisp individual fur fiber rendering.
Audio: Howling mountain wind, soft crunch of packed snow under paws, low predatory rumble.
```

## 2. Volumetric Supercell Storm Formation

```text
Model: Runway Gen-3 / Kling 3.0 | Aspect: 2.35:1 | Duration: 8s | FPS: 24

A colossal rotating supercell thunderstorm sweeps across golden wheat fields in the American Midwest at sunset.
Intense anvil cloud tops billow into the upper stratosphere, internal lightning discharging in pale violet pulses.
The low-hanging dark mesocyclone rotates with immense scale and atmospheric power.
Camera: Hyperlapse camera drifting slowly forward across the golden wheat plains toward the approaching storm wall.
Look: Dramatic twilight contrast, golden setting sunlight illuminating storm edge clouds against ink-black rain curtains.
Audio: Continuous deep rolling thunder rumble, rushing wind sweeping through wheat fields.
```

## 3. Rainforest Tree Frog Water Drop Macro

```text
Model: Kling 3.0 / Seedance 2.0 | Aspect: 1:1 | Duration: 6s | FPS: 60

An extreme macro view of a vibrant red-eyed tree frog perched on a glossy tropical monstera leaf.
A large spherical raindrop falls from above, landing directly on the leaf next to the frog in ultra-slow motion (60fps).
The droplet deforms into a liquid crown splash, sending microscopic water spheres refracting ambient light.
Camera: Extreme Close-Up (ECU) locked macro framing with 100mm macro lens, depth of field razor-thin on frog's iris.
Look: Soft diffused jungle canopy illumination, vibrant emerald greens and saturated frog skin pigments.
Audio: Amplified macro liquid droplet impact sound, lush ambient rainforest bird calls in background.
```
