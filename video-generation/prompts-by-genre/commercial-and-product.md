# Production Prompts: Commercial & Product Showcase

High-converting commercial prompt templates for luxury goods, tech hardware, cosmetics, apparel, and beverage commercials.

---

## 1. Luxury Chronograph Watch Macro Reveal

```text
Model: Kling 3.0 / Seedance 2.0 | Aspect: 16:9 | Duration: 6s | FPS: 30

An immaculate stainless steel mechanical chronograph watch rests on dark, volcanic slate stone.
Fine water droplets bead across the beveled sapphire crystal face.
The central chronometer second hand sweeps continuously with mechanical precision across sunburst dial subdials.
Camera: Slow Robo Arm arc sweeping diagonally from 45-degree bevel edge over the sapphire glass to an Extreme Close-Up.
Look: Commercial luxury lighting, dual rim softboxes creating razor-sharp linear highlights on polished metal, F2.8 macro.
Audio: Clean mechanical ticking of an automatic movement, crisp metallic click, deep ambient luxury sub-bass swell.
```

## 2. Athletic Footwear Gravity Drop

```text
Model: Kling 3.0 | Aspect: 9:16 | Duration: 5s | FPS: 60

A high-performance neon-lime running shoe suspended in mid-air against a seamless matte dark-grey cyclorama.
A pressurized cloud of fine colored powder (magenta and cyan) explodes outward from the sole cushion as if upon impact.
Every thread of engineered mesh and rubber lug is rendered with tactile clarity.
Camera: Crash Zoom In from full shoe profile to extreme macro on the compressed foam sole cushioning.
Look: High-speed studio flash lighting, 1/2000s freeze-frame clarity, rich color saturation on dynamic powder particles.
Audio: Massive acoustic sub-bass boom on impact, crisp granular whoosh of dispersing powder.
```

## 3. Premium Espresso Pour & Crema

```text
Model: Kling 3.0 / Seedance 2.0 | Aspect: 1:1 | Duration: 6s | FPS: 30

A double spout portafilter pours rich, viscous hazelnut espresso in dual smooth streams into a ceramic cup.
Golden crema swirls and forms a marbled pattern on the surface.
Gentle steam wisps curl upward into the warm overhead light.
Camera: Locked-off macro framing, 100mm macro lens, focus plane locked strictly on the pouring espresso stream.
Look: Warm morning cafe lighting, rich coffee tones, soft golden backlight catching rising steam particles.
Audio: Gentle rhythmic hiss of espresso extraction, delicate liquid splashing into porcelain cup.
```
