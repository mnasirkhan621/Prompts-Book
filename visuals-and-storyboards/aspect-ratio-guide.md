# Aspect Ratio & Geometric Framing Standards
### *Platform Specifications and Compositional Guides*
**Architect:** **Muhammad Nasir Khan**

Aspect ratios dictate compositional balance and viewer psychology. Setting exact aspect ratios in video prompts ensures visual fidelity across target display formats.

---

## 1. Standard Aspect Ratios Matrix

| Aspect Ratio | Decimal Ratio | Standard Resolution | Native Platform | Emotional Framing Psychology |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **16:9** | 1.78:1 | 1920×1080 / 3840×2160 | YouTube, Television, Desktop | Standard expansive horizon, balanced horizontal space |
| **9:16** | 0.56:1 | 1080×1920 | TikTok, Instagram Reels, Shorts | Vertical intimacy, mobile immersion, centered single subject |
| **2.39:1** | 2.39:1 | 2560×1072 / 3840×1606 | Cinema Theatrical, Anamorphic | Epic scope, vast horizontal vistas, cinematic prestige |
| **4:3** | 1.33:1 | 1440×1080 | Vintage Television, Classic Film | Nostalgic retro, claustrophobic portraiture, intimacy |
| **1:1** | 1.00:1 | 1080×1080 | Social Feeds, Product Grid | Symmetry, balanced square containment, product focus |
