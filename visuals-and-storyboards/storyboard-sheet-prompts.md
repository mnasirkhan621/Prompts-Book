# Storyboard Contact Sheet Prompts for Video Pre-Production

Before generating video clips, generate consistent 6-panel or 9-panel storyboard contact sheets using image models (GPT Image 2, Midjourney v6/v7, or Flux). This locks character identities, set architecture, and color palettes across scenes.

---

## 6-Panel Cinematic Storyboard Prompt

```text
A professional cinematic storyboard sheet featuring a 6-panel grid layout (2 rows of 3 panels), 16:9 aspect ratio per panel.
Clean dark charcoal borders between frames, with small white frame labels "Frame 1" through "Frame 6" below each box.
Unified visual aesthetic: 35mm film stock, cold steel-blue shadows, warm amber tungsten key light, high contrast neo-noir.
Consistent character: A sharp-eyed woman in a tailored dark trench coat with a high collar.
- Panel 1: Extreme Wide Shot (EWS) of a rainy dockland at midnight. Solitary figure walking beneath a rusty crane.
- Panel 2: Medium Shot (MS) profile of the woman turning toward camera, rain dripping from coat lapels.
- Panel 3: Close-Up (CU) on her hands opening a weathered brass pocket compass glowing faintly blue.
- Panel 4: Low-Angle Cowboy Shot (MLS) as two silhouetted guards approach through dense harbor fog.
- Panel 5: Dynamic Action Shot of the woman darting behind stacked cargo containers as flashlight beams sweep by.
- Panel 6: Centered Over-The-Shoulder (OTS) look into a secret trapdoor revealing stairs descending into warm light.
Ultra-high detail, professional film director pre-visualization storyboard sheet.
```

## 9-Panel Commercial Product Storyboard Prompt

```text
A high-end commercial advertising storyboard sheet, 9-panel grid (3x3 layout), 1:1 square aspect ratio per frame.
Minimalist matte grey background with subtle white dividers.
Product focus: A matte-black wireless over-ear headphone with copper acoustic mesh accents.
- Panel 1: Extreme Close-Up (ECU) of copper mesh texture under a dramatic rim light.
- Panel 2: Macro profile of the precision aluminum volume knob rotating smoothly.
- Panel 3: Exploded view showing internal acoustic chamber and neodymium drivers.
- Panel 4: Side profile of a model wearing the headphones, closed eyes, lost in sound.
- Panel 5: Wide Shot of the model standing on a busy subway platform, background blurred in motion.
- Panel 6: Close-Up of water droplets beading off the splash-resistant headphone band.
- Panel 7: Floating hero angle of headphones suspended in zero gravity against a dark gradient.
- Panel 8: Headphones folding neatly into a compact magnetic leather travel case.
- Panel 9: Clean centered typography lockup: "AURA ACOUSTICS. SILENCE THE WORLD."
Clean, photorealistic commercial product pre-visualization sheet.
```
